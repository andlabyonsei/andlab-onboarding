# Purchasing with Indirect Costs

How to buy lab items with indirect research costs (indirect costs of the Intelligent Innovation Research Institute, 지능형혁신연구소), and **when and how to send the supporting documents to the administrator** after purchase.

::: info Institute administrator (from 2026.09.01)
**배유림** — [byr@yonsei.ac.kr](mailto:byr@yonsei.ac.kr)

Ask this administrator about whether indirect costs can be used, submitting payment documents, and settlement. Communication is in Korean — ask a labmate for help if needed.
:::

## At a Glance

| Step | What to do | When |
|------|-------|------|
| 1. Check first | **Discuss with your advisor** and get approval, then send the item, amount, and purpose to the administrator to confirm **eligibility, whether it counts as equipment, remaining limit, and deadline** | **Before** payment |
| 2. Order & pay | Get the **shared card and ISP USB** from the administrator and pay on the **PC at the Finance & Accounting Team in Baekyang Hall** (weekdays) | After confirmation |
| 3. Receive & photograph | Take **2 photos** of each item | Right after delivery |
| 4. Submit documents | Email the **receipts and item photos together** to the administrator, and fill out the **delivery confirmation form** for your advisor | After receiving the items |


## 1. Before Purchasing

### Discuss with your advisor

Indirect costs are your advisor's budget, so first **discuss and get approval from your advisor** for the **items, quantity, approximate amount, and why they're needed**. After approval, check the points below and contact the administrator.

### Eligible uses

The institute's indirect costs are a budget for running the institute and supporting research and academic activities.

- Institute operating costs, space maintenance costs
- Publication fees, internationalization expenses, English proofreading fees
- Office supplies and shared items

**Equipment for individual research projects** of a specific lab, or **items for personal use**, may not be eligible.

### Equipment purchase limit

Equipment (비품) can only be bought within the general-purpose equipment limit set by the Office of Research, **separately** from the indirect cost balance.

- The equipment limit is an **institute-wide limit shared** by the institute's professors, managed by the institute based on each professor's allowance.
- Even if the indirect cost balance is sufficient, this separate limit applies to equipment.
- Equipment must be **general-purpose items that institute members can use in common**.
- The limit **changes with each allocation**, so ask the administrator for the current remaining limit before purchasing.

### Deadline

The deadline for using indirect costs and whether they can be carried over **depend on the fiscal year and allocation timing.** Ask the administrator about the current budget's deadline before purchasing.

::: warning Always check before paying
Equipment purchases may require prior steps such as budget reallocation or equipment registration. **Before paying**, email the administrator the **items, amount, and purpose** to confirm whether they count as equipment, the remaining limit, and the deadline.
:::


## 2. Ordering & Payment

- Using the **institute's shared card (corporate card)** is recommended. Paying with the director professor's corporate card is also possible, but then an extra department transfer step is needed. ([see below](#professor-corporate-card))
- **Avoid using the card on weekends and holidays.** If unavoidable, include a **reason of 10 characters or less** (in Korean).
- For items with components such as desktops, the administrator may ask you to **order parts like SSDs and RAM separately from the main unit, classified as consumables**. Decide how to order with the administrator during the check step.

### Paying with the institute's shared card

Online payments with the shared card require an **ISP certificate (USB)**, and payment is made directly on a PC at the [**Finance & Accounting Team in Baekyang Hall**](https://www.yonsei.ac.kr/sc/359/subview.do?enc=Zm5jdDF8QEB8JTJGY3JkTG5rJTJGc2MlMkYxNiUyRjMxJTJGdmlldy5kbyUzRg%3D%3D).

1. Contact the administrator to get the **shared card and ISP USB**.
2. Go to the Yonsei University Finance & Accounting Team. The team starts work at **9 AM**, so visit after that.
3. Order and pay **directly on the team's PC**.
4. After paying, return the card and ISP USB to the administrator.

::: tip
To pay right away on the team's PC, prepare the **product links, quantities, and shipping address** before your visit.
:::


## 3. Photographing Items After Delivery

For settlement, **2 photos per item** are required.

| Situation | How to photograph |
|------|-----------|
| Default | **1 photo of the box + 1 photo of the item** |
| No box | **2 photos of the item from different angles** |
| Several of the same item | Make sure **all of them appear in one photo** (e.g. 2 monitors, 3 chairs) |
| Sets | Photograph each set separately |
| SSD/RAM installed in a desktop | A photo of the case isn't enough → **open the case cover and take a close-up of the installed parts** |

::: tip
Even if ordered separately, SSDs and RAM may arrive already installed in the desktop. Taking photos of the boxes and parts before assembly saves you from opening the case again later.
:::


## 4. Submitting Documents

After receiving the items, send **the receipts and item photos together in one email** to the administrator. Separately, **fill out the delivery confirmation form and give it to your advisor**.

### Delivery confirmation form for your advisor

After receiving the items, fill out the **delivery confirmation form (납품증빙서류)** and **give it to your advisor**. Download the form below. If you're unsure how to fill it out, ask the administrator.

| Form | Download |
|------|----------|
| Delivery confirmation form (for your advisor) | <a href="/docs/purchasing/delivery-proof-form.hwp" download="납품증빙서류_양식.hwp">Download HWP (Korean)</a> |

::: info
HWP is a Korean document format that opens with [Hancom Office](https://www.hancom.com/). Ask a labmate if you can't open it.
:::

### Documents to submit

| Document | Notes |
|------|------|
| Card receipt | Corporate card payment receipt |
| Statement of transaction / purchase receipt | Issued by the seller |
| Order details screen | **For online purchases (Naver, etc.)**, attach the order details (showing product name, amount paid, and payment method) as a PDF or screenshot |
| Item photos | 2 per item (per step 3 above) |
| Reason for weekend/holiday use | If applicable, 10 characters or less |

Attaching multiple files as a zip is convenient.

### Email example

The administrator communicates in Korean, so send the email in Korean using the template below.

```
받는 사람: 배유림 선생님 (byr@yonsei.ac.kr)
제목: [ANDlab] MM/DD 결제 건 증빙 자료 전달드립니다.

배유림 선생님, 안녕하세요. 정보대학원 장백철 교수님 ANDlab 연구실의 OOO입니다.
MM/DD에 결제한 건의 증빙을 위해 카드영수증, 구매영수증, 주문상세내역, 물품 사진을 첨부하여 보내드립니다.
(구매 품목: 데스크톱 2대, 모니터 2대 …)
확인하신 뒤, 결제 내역에 문제가 없는지 검토 부탁드립니다.

감사합니다.
```

:::

### Paid with the director professor's corporate card {#professor-corporate-card}

A **department transfer** to the institute budget is needed. Send the **payment date** along with the **card receipt and statement of transaction** to the administrator, who will process the transfer after checking.
