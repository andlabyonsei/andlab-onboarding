# Lab Waste Disposal

The procedure for disposing of items from the lab.

Before disposing of anything, first check for an **asset sticker (school property)**. The procedure depends on whether the item has one.

## Summary

| Type | Condition | How to dispose |
|------|------|-----------|
| School property | Has an asset sticker | Email photos to the department office (`gsi@yonsei.ac.kr`) and wait for instructions |
| General items | No asset sticker | Dispose of it yourself in the lab (on-campus disposal vendor available) |


## 1. Items with an Asset Sticker (School Property)

Items registered as school property cannot be disposed of at will.

1. Prepare the following photos for each item:
   - A photo of the **asset sticker**
   - A photo of the **whole item**
2. Email the photos for each item to the **department office (`gsi@yonsei.ac.kr`)**.
3. Follow the office's instructions for the next steps.

::: warning
Do not dispose of items with an asset sticker yourself without the office's confirmation.
:::


## 2. Items without an Asset Sticker

Items without an asset sticker can be **disposed of within the lab**.  
For large items or large quantities, use the on-campus disposal vendor below.

### Vendor

| Item | Details |
|------|------|
| Vendor | 복천 (Bokcheon Co., resident on campus) |
| Working hours | 6 AM – 3 PM |

::: tip
Vendor contact details are on the [facility repair · disposal · unused-item request page](https://chemeng.yonsei.ac.kr/?c=211&s=211&gp=1&gbn=viewok&ix=2863) (Korean).
:::

### Requesting disposal

1. **Send a work request by text message** including:
   - **Requester**: name, email, phone number
   - **Affiliation**: building name on campus, lab room number
   - **List of items to dispose of** (attach images)
   - **Preferred dates**
   - **Questions**, if any (disassembly, moving out of the building, etc.)
2. The vendor replies with the **cost** and **available dates**.
3. Once the date and cost are confirmed, proceed as instructed.

::: info
The vendor handles all disposal work on campus, so **it may take longer than your preferred date.** Request with plenty of time to spare.
:::
