# Webmaster Duties

The ANDlab website runs on **Google Sites**. The webmaster edits and updates the lab site on the lab's behalf.

## How to Access

1. Sign in with the **professor's school Google account**.  
   - Get the account details from **the professor or the previous webmaster**.  
   - For security, the password is not recorded in this guide.

2. Go to the [Google Sites editor link](https://sites.google.com/d/1umIvDCAunz90KAgzTj6J99ZjNyr9QP-6/p/1dMuKkOy1eYzgiQkDRyVgQ9l2AjR6dBTf/edit?pli=1).

3. You can also edit from the Google app launcher (the **3×3 grid** next to the profile image) → **Sites** app.


## Publications Ordering Rules

1. Place **international papers** above **domestic (Korean) papers**
2. Within each group, sort **newest first** (most recent paper on top)


## Member Ordering Rules

1. Put the **lab lead** at the top
2. Everyone else in **ascending order of admission** (most recent admits at the bottom)
   - Applies equally to master's and PhD students
3. Full-time members first, then part-time


## Checklist

- [ ] Signed in with the professor's account?
- [ ] Kept the Publications order (international > domestic, newest first)?
- [ ] Kept the member order?
- [ ] Checked the layout with the preview?
