# Lab Door Access

The ANDlab door uses both a student ID card reader and a KT access system. Follow the steps below exactly so the door opens and locks properly.

## Opening the Door (First Person In)

1. Press the **"해제" (Disarm)** button, then **tap your student ID card on the reader**.
2. Once the door opens, go inside and press the **white KT button** to disable auto-lock.

::: tip
If you don't press the KT button, the door locks again — press it right after you come in.
:::

## Locking the Door (Last Person Out)

1. Press the **white KT button** first to extend the lock.
2. **Shortly after**, press the black pad.
3. Open the door and step out. The door locks automatically after **about 3 seconds**.
4. Press the **"경계" (Arm)** button, then **tap your student ID card on the reader**.

::: warning Caution
- Before leaving, keep the order: KT button → black pad. If the order is reversed, the door may not lock.
- The last person to leave the lab must always lock the door using the steps above.
:::
